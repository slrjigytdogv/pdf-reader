Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the PDF Reader desktop app.

Is PDF Reader really free?
Yes. pdf-reader is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no premium tiers, watermarks, or feature locks — every feature is included.
Which operating systems are supported?
Native builds are available for Windows 10 and 11, macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux distributions including Ubuntu, Fedora, and Arch.
Does it handle very large PDF files?
Yes. PDF Reader uses lazy page loading and hardware-accelerated rendering, so documents with thousands of pages open quickly and scroll smoothly without freezing.
Are my annotations and bookmarks saved?
Absolutely. Annotations are written back into the PDF file itself, and bookmarks are stored in a local sidecar file next to the document — so they travel with your files.
